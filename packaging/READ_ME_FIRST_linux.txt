BibleChat for Linux: how to install
===================================

EASIEST: double-click "Install BibleChat" in this folder.
   If your computer asks what to do, choose "Launch Anyway", "Mark as Trusted",
   "Run" or "Run in Terminal" (the words differ between Linux versions).
   A window shows what happens. BibleChat then appears in your applications
   menu and on your desktop, and it starts by itself.

OR, IN A TERMINAL (in this folder), type exactly this and press Enter:

       ./install.sh

   Note the dot and the slash at the start: Linux only runs a program from the
   current folder when you write ./ in front of it. Typing just  install.sh
   gives "command not found". This works too:   sh install.sh

TRY IT WITHOUT INSTALLING: double-click BibleChat/BibleChat, or type
       ./BibleChat/BibleChat

AFTER INSTALLING, always open BibleChat from your applications menu or the desktop
icon. You can delete this download folder and any older BibleChat folders: the
installed copy is the one that counts, and installing a new version replaces it.

When BibleChat opens in your browser, click "Get free Bibles" to download a Bible.
Nothing you read or ask is sent anywhere; everything stays on this computer.
