About BibleChat. Read and search free Bibles right here, with no sign-up and nothing to install. Tap your browser's Share → Add to Home Screen to keep it like an app.

On a computer, the full BibleChat app adds AI explanations, voice questions and your own Bible files.

BibleChat is made by Hans W. Beaugrand. Free and open source under the Apache License 2.0; anyone who shares or builds on it keeps this credit (NOTICE). Each Bible keeps its own license, shown in the Bibles tab.

⛪ For churches

Put your church's name and logo in BibleChat, then show or print a QR code. Everyone who scans it opens the free app with your church's name at the top.

Pastors: tap a verse, then 📋 Add to sermon. On Sunday show the list with its QR code, so people follow along and take the verses home.
