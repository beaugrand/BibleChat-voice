# BibleChat privacy policy

*Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Contact: Hans W. Beaugrand, [github.com/beaugrand/BibleChat-voice/issues](https://github.com/beaugrand/BibleChat-voice/issues)*

BibleChat (the phone version, the computer app and the BibleChat connector for AI apps) is built to collect nothing about you. The phone version counts visits, without knowing who you are (see below).

- **What we collect:** nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads and no cookies. The only thing counted is visits to the phone version (next point).
- **🔢 Visit counter (phone version only):** each time BibleChat is opened at beaugrand.github.io, it tells [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com) (a free, open-source counter) that it was opened, whether from the home screen, and the website you came from (only its name, such as facebook.com, never the full address). GoatCounter uses your internet address once to find your country and then throws the address away; it sets no cookies and keeps no profile of you. The total number of visits is shown at the bottom of Home and in Love → About. To not be counted, switch off **Count my visits** in Love → About; that phone is then never counted. The computer app and the connector count nothing.
- **What stays on your device:** your Bibles, bookmarks, highlights, chats, settings, sermon lists and your church's name and logo are kept only on your own phone or computer.
- **What leaves your device, only when you ask:** downloading a free Bible (from the Bible's public source); the computer app's once-a-day check with GitHub for a newer version; links you choose to open (an AI site under "Ask AI", Cash App, email for suggestions). Those services have their own privacy policies.
- **🎙 Tap and talk (phone version):** only when you tap the microphone, your browser's own speech recognition listens. Your browser sends your spoken words to its maker (Google, Apple or Microsoft) to turn them into text, under that company's privacy policy. BibleChat keeps no recording; the text becomes a Bible search on your phone, and the answer is read aloud by your phone's own voice.
- **The connector (MCP server):** it answers a request (for example a Bible reference) and forgets it. It keeps no logs, stores no requests and shares nothing with anyone. When it runs on your own computer it can read the Bibles you added to BibleChat there; the public version uses only built-in public-domain data.
- **Third-party sharing:** none, apart from the visit counter above (GoatCounter, under [its own privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy)). We do not sell or share data.
- **Data retention:** we retain nothing about you; GoatCounter keeps only the visit counts. To remove what is on your device, delete BibleChat's data folder or clear the site's data in your browser.
- **Children:** BibleChat collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
- **Changes:** changes to this policy are published in this file.
