# BibleChat privacy policy

*Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Contact: Hans W. Beaugrand, [github.com/beaugrand/BibleChat-voice/issues](https://github.com/beaugrand/BibleChat-voice/issues)*

BibleChat (the phone version, the computer app and the BibleChat connector for AI apps) is built to collect nothing about you. The only thing it counts is how often it is used, anonymously.

- **What we collect:** nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads and no cookies, and nothing follows you to other sites.
- **The visit counter:** BibleChat counts how often it is opened and which parts are used (for example Read, Search, Study Paths, Listen, a highlight made), so its maker can see how many people it reaches. It uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com) (free and open source). GoatCounter receives the name of the part used, the screen width, the browser and the site a visitor came from, and works out the country; it keeps no IP address and sets no cookies ([GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy)). It never receives a verse, a search, a question, a note, a highlight's text, a chat or a study path. To leave yourself out: on the phone, untick **Count my visits** under Love → About; on the computer, untick it in the ♥ window.
- **What stays on your device:** your Bibles, bookmarks, highlights, chats, settings, sermon lists and your church's name and logo are kept only on your own phone or computer.
- **What leaves your device, only when you ask:** downloading a free Bible (from the Bible's public source); the computer app's once-a-day check with GitHub for a newer version; links you choose to open (an AI site under "Ask AI", Cash App, email for suggestions). Those services have their own privacy policies.
- **The connector (MCP server):** it answers a request (for example a Bible reference) and forgets it. It keeps no logs, stores no requests and shares nothing with anyone. When it runs on your own computer it can read the Bibles you added to BibleChat there; the public version uses only built-in public-domain data.
- **Third-party sharing:** none, apart from the anonymous visit counter above. We do not sell or share data.
- **Data retention:** we retain nothing about you; the visit counter keeps only totals. To remove what is on your device, delete BibleChat's data folder or clear the site's data in your browser.
- **Children:** BibleChat collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
- **Changes:** changes to this policy are published in this file.
